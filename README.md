# PRIVACY POLICY FOR TURN BLOCK!

*Last updated: October 29, 2026*

Developed by MaLei. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you play our game.

### 1. NO PERSONAL INFORMATION COLLECTED
"Turn Block!" is a premium, minimalist puzzle game. We **DO NOT** collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) from our users, such as your name, email address, phone number, physical location, or device contacts.

### 2. NON-PERSONAL AND ANALYTICS DATA
To improve gameplay experience, resolve technical bugs, and balance level progression, we use third-party analytics services (**specifically, GameAnalytics**). These tools collect purely non-personal, aggregated gameplay data, including:
- Game progression, score achievements, and combo statistics.
- Raccoon Boss levels cleared or failed.
- Device type, operating system version, and basic performance metrics.
This data is completely anonymized and cannot be used to identify or contact you.

### 3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
Our analytics provider processes data strictly in accordance with international privacy standards (including COPPA and GDPR compliance). You can review their privacy practices at: https://gameanalytics.com

### 4. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Since we do not collect any personal information, "Turn Block!" is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and is safe for users of all ages.

### 5. CONTACT US
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us via our developer support channels.
